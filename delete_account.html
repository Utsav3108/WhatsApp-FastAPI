How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Ripple account and all associated personal data directly from inside the app at any time. Follow these steps:

  1. Open the Ripple app on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to your Profile tab (the right-most icon in the bottom navigation bar).
  3. Scroll down to the bottom of the Profile page.
  4. Click the red Delete Account button.
  5. A confirmation dialog will appear, detailing the permanent removal of your data. Click Delete to confirm.

Once confirmed, your account session is invalidated, and your database records are erased immediately and permanently.

Alternative Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the Ripple application, do not have access to your mobile device, or want to make a request manually, you can contact the developer, Utsav Pandya, to delete your account:

  • Email: send an email request to pandyautsav3108@gmail.com.
  • Requirement: Please send the email from the Google account you used to register in the Ripple app so we can verify your identity.
  • Processing Time: Manual deletion requests are processed within 2-3 business days. You will receive an email confirmation once the process is complete.

What Data is Deleted or Retained?

We believe in absolute user privacy. When you request account deletion (either via the app or via email), the following actions take place:

Data That is Permanently Deleted:

  • Profile Information: Your name, email, professional/personal role, biography context, and avatar image link.
  • Chat History: All conversation messages, including prompts sent to personas and responses received from them.
  • Game History: All challenge attempts, challenge sessions, scores, durations, and outcomes.
  • Moderation Reports: Any safety feedback, moderation flags, or reports associated with your account's chats.

Data That is Kept:

  • None. We do not retain any of your personal identifiers, chat history, or strategy records after deletion.

Retention Period:

All data is purged instantly when triggered inside the app. For manual email requests, data is permanently purged within 72 hours of verification.
